How to read this

Values are millimetres of water in the snowpack at the station, not areal averages. The line is the median forecast, the shaded band the 80% range (split-conformal on held-out years), the dashed line the station's median for that week. Forecasts begin in November; outside the November-June season this page shows the latest status only. Stations that did not report in the issue week get no forecast. Skill is measured on stations with at least 15 good water years and on station-years with at least 90% complete winter data. Horizons beyond 13 weeks are not shown: the seasonal median was better than the model there. Source: PhreAItic; methods and the plateau analysis in the project repository (projects/snow-stores).
