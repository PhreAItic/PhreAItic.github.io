# PhreAItic

> Forecasts of groundwater levels, rivers and lakes, river temperature and snow for 46 countries (40,335 monitoring stations), 1 to 12 months ahead, with calibrated 80% bands, graded on years the models never saw.

Read-only JSON API, no key, GET only, CORS open, rebuilt about weekly. Base: https://phreaitic.github.io/api/v1

## How to use it (three requests cover most questions)
- Near a place: GET https://phreaitic.github.io/api/v1/grid/<floor(lat)>_<floor(lon)>.json  (Brno 49.19N 16.61E -> grid/49_16.json). Pick the station with the smallest distance; also check neighbouring cells near a cell edge.
- A country and layer: GET https://phreaitic.github.io/api/v1/<CC>/<layer>.json  (CC = ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 such as CZ, US, FR; layer = gw, rivers, temp, snow).
- Trust and caveats: GET https://phreaitic.github.io/api/v1/live_record.json and read each network's backtest_by_horizon_months.

## Reading a forecast
- median = forecast; q10..q90 = 80% band; pct = percentile of the median among the same time of year in that station's own history (below 20 low for the season, above 80 high).
- model_served false means the stronger simple baseline (last value or seasonal normal) is what the map shows, not the model.
- The nearest station is not the user's exact spot. Say the distance. Do not present these as safety-critical advice.
- Each network carries its source and licence; attribute the source when you reuse a number.
